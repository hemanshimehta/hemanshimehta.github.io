03EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS

Exploring future thinking in dementia

MSc dissertation · University College London

A PRISMA-guided systematic review of episodic future thinking in dementia, bringing together evidence from five academic databases.

24 studiesin the final synthesis · dissertation: 72%
My contribution & approach

Search. Medline, PsycINFO, Embase, CINAHL and Web of Science; 975 records identified.

My contribution. Screened 536 records using Covidence and synthesised 24 studies following PRISMA guidance.

Quality assessment. Applied the Newcastle–Ottawa Scale. Awarded Distinction for the dissertation.
